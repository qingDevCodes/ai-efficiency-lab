Rewrite the delay notice below as three complete emails I can send as-is:

A) Formal
B) Warm
C) Firm-but-polite

Same facts, three stances. Hard rules for all three:

1. Use only the facts in the notice: order number, customer, quantity, promised date, actual date, and the offer. Every version must contain every one of those facts, unchanged.
2. No new promises. Do not add a delivery ETA, tracking timeline, future discount, process change, "this won't happen again", or any commitment that is not in the notice.
3. Do not invent a reason for the delay. If the notice gives no customer-facing reason, give none.
4. Lines starting with `internal:` are instructions for you. Follow them, never quote or paraphrase them to the customer.
5. Each email is complete: Subject line, greeting, body, sign-off. Sign with the sender named in the notice; if none, use `[Your name]`.
6. Max 120 words per email body.
7. The tone difference must come from wording and stance, not from changing facts or the offer:
   - Formal: neutral and precise, no contractions, no exclamation marks.
   - Warm: human, acknowledges the inconvenience once, contractions OK, no gushing.
   - Firm-but-polite: owns the delay once, states the offer plainly and its scope, no over-apologizing, no softeners like "just" or "hopefully".
